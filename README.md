Embedded Enthusiast

🚀 About Me

Hi, I’m Pushkar Lokhande (@Pushkar4568), an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student passionate about Embedded Systems, Microcontrollers, and Bare-Metal Programming. I love working close to the hardware and exploring the capabilities of STM32 boards (M0, M4) to build efficient, low-level solutions.

💡 What I Do

Experimenting with embedded C, registers, and hardware abstraction

Building projects around STM32, ESP32, and sensor integration

Exploring IoT applications, real-time systems, and low-power designs

Interested in avionics, automotive, and semiconductor technologies

🤝 Collaboration Goals

I’m looking to collaborate on projects that need an electronics/embedded engineer—whether it’s firmware development, sensor-based systems, or prototyping hardware with STMicroelectronics chips.

🌱 Currently Learning

Bare-metal firmware design for Cortex-M processors

Interfacing sensors and communication protocols (I2C, SPI, UART)

Fundamentals of real-time operating systems (RTOS)
